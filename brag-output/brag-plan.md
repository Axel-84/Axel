# Brag Plan: CO3101 CW1 · Question 3

## What is this app?
A critical evaluation of a class padlet on inclusive software engineering: 45 posts sorted into five themes by Copilot, then checked post by post — which caught four posts Copilot had filed in the wrong theme.

## The angle
Not "look at my mind map" but "look at what the AI got wrong, and how I caught it". The total was correct (45), so a quick check would have passed it. Reading every post found four misfiled ones, and all four errors pointed the same way: Copilot made the padlet look more positive than it is.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
45 numbered post chips (P1…P45) rain into a grid. Headline: "Copilot sorted 45 padlet posts." Second line: "I checked every one."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Copilot's first grouping as five bars with the real theme names and its first counts: 13 · 12 · 12 · 2 · 6. Total 45, tick. "The total was correct."
- Ground flips dark: "But four posts were in the wrong theme." The four real posts land as cards with their own words: P8 "if handled well", P43 "not inherently bad", P22 "reach bigger target audiences", P31 "pressure on companies / businesses".
- The bars correct themselves: 13→12, 12→11, 12→11, 2→4, 6→7. "Copilot made the padlet look more positive than it is."
- The real Figure 1 mind map, in full.

## Outro / punchline
"Number every post. Check every post." over the mind map's centre blue, with "CO3101 CW1 · Question 3".

## User flow worth showing
Number the 45 posts → Copilot groups them → manual check finds 4 wrong → corrected counts → mind map.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: a calm, exact audit film — light paper ground, one dark beat for the catch
- Interpretation: comfortable pacing, real numbers and quotes only, no jokes added; the reveal carries it.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23.5 seconds

- Background: #ffffff (the light-theme mind map)
- Accent: #0000ec (mind map centre node)
- Text: #111122

- Body / post-number font: IBM Plex Mono
- Strongest visual element: Figure1-mindmap.png

## Privacy
No student, lecturer or university names, no padlet URL, no chat links. Post text is anonymous class input, quoted only where the written answer already quotes it.

## Share copy (draft)
Copilot sorted 45 padlet posts and the total added up. I read every post anyway and found four in the wrong theme.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady and clean), from 0s
- Music treatment: 0.32 volume, short fade-in, fade out under the final line
- Music cue guidance: preset read (110 BPM). Strong cues used: 8.74s (the catch), 16.38s (mind map), 19.66s (outro). Beat grid 9.29–10.93s for the four post cards (every beat is fine: cards are held together afterwards).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music energy sets the strength of the glow behind the "45" total and the outro halo. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse; card sounds on the four posts, one soft impact on the catch, one bell on the outro
- Audio-coupled moments: four cards on the beat; bar corrections land together on 13.11s
- Restraint rule: nothing harsh, nothing on every chip of the opening grid

# Q3 video script (about 2.5 minutes)

**Open before recording:** the Copilot chat (scrolled to the top), the Excel padlet file, your final Word page, and `Figure1-mindmap.png`.
**Record with:** Teams (Meeting → Share screen → Record), PowerPoint (Insert → Screen Recording) or OBS. Zoom the browser to 125%.

| Time | Show on screen | Say (in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Word page, Question 3 | "Hi, I'm [name]. I did Question 3, the critical evaluation of the inclusive software engineering padlet. I also helped with [anything you did on Q1/Q2, or remove this]." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Excel file | "The padlet had 45 posts. The export splits each post into a subject and a body, with 'No data' when one is empty, so I merged them and numbered them P1 to P45 before giving them to Copilot. I removed nothing else, and there were no names." |
| 0:35–1:00 | Copilot: Prompt 1 and its answer | "I used Copilot with my university account. I asked it to group the posts into themes, give the post numbers and counts, and not invent anything. It made five themes and the counts added up to 45." |
| 1:00–1:30 | Copilot's answer: point at P8, P43, P22, P31 | "But I checked every post myself and found four mistakes. P8 says diversity helps only 'if handled well', and P43 says non-diverse teams aren't inherently bad. Those are cautious posts, but Copilot listed them as benefits. P22 and P31 are about business, but it put them elsewhere, so Business had only 2 posts. That made the padlet look more positive than it is." |
| 1:30–1:50 | Your correction prompt → Copilot's mind map | "So I re-prompted it with the corrections, checked the new counts (12, 11, 11, 4 and 7), and asked it for a mind map as Mermaid code. It was dark and hard to read, so it was re-drawn in a light theme with exactly the same content." |
| 1:50–2:20 | Word page: "Who is missing" box | "My main findings: almost every post is positive, and only 7 of 45 are cautious. No post cites any evidence. Disabled people, women and low-income users are mentioned once each, and older, neurodivergent and LGBTQ+ users are never mentioned, even though the padlet is about impacts on people. I compared the posts with outside evidence like the Gender Shades study and McKinsey, and noted that McKinsey only shows correlation." |
| 2:20–2:40 | References page | "AI use: Copilot grouped the posts and made the mind map. I checked and corrected it. Claude helped me analyse the posts, find sources, draft the wording and re-draw the mind map. I checked every quote and number against the padlet. Both chats are linked in the references." |

**Tips:** don't read word for word. Practise it twice and speak naturally. Be ready for a question like "why is P43 a limitation?" and answer it in your own words.
